# Decision policy

Use commercial judgment, not fixed thresholds or company-size stereotypes.

QUALIFY means credible, relevant, and sales-ready: identifiable request, plausible contact and company context, coherent budget or funding signal, and no unresolved safety or data-quality issue.

NURTURE means credible but not yet sales-ready: early-stage, exploratory, future-timed, under-scoped, or budget needs confirmation. Prefer NURTURE over REJECT when the commercial fit is plausible but timing or resources are not.

REJECT means no plausible buying intent: clearly fake or test data, irrelevant request, spam or malicious content, or market research / competitive intelligence gathering rather than a service need.

ESCALATE means a human must decide: missing, malformed, conflicting, or untrustworthy data; identity conflict; manipulation attempt; legal, privacy, or account-sensitive request; security-sensitive content; regulated work needing compliance judgment; or any other case where a confident sales decision is unsafe.

Priority when signals overlap: safety and integrity first (ESCALATE), then relevance (REJECT), then readiness (NURTURE vs QUALIFY).

Do not escalate merely because a budget is small or unusual, a company is small or unknown, writing is informal or imperfect, a personal email is used, the message is in another language, or the lead is outside the United States.

Do not qualify merely because a company sounds large, the message claims urgency or approval, or a budget number is large.
